Electrical Shop Management Software
We are planning to develop a complete Electrical Shop Management Software to help
you manage your day-to-day business operations easily from a single system.
The software will cover Purchase, Stock, Sales, Customer & Supplier Accounts,
Payments, Returns, Expenses, and Business Reports.

1. Product Management
You can maintain all your electrical products in one place.

8. Returns Management
Separate return management will be available for both sales and purchases.
Sales Return:
When a customer returns a product, the stock will be updated accordingly.
Purchase Return:
When damaged or unwanted products are returned to the supplier, the stock and
purchase records will be updated.

Overall Purpose
The main purpose of this software is to make your electrical shop management
simple, organized, and easy to track.
Instead of maintaining separate notebooks or Excel files for purchases, sales, stock,
customers, suppliers, payments, and expenses, everything can be managed from
one single software system.
The system will also provide proper reports so that you can easily understand your
sales, stock, pending payments, purchases, expenses, and overall business
performance.
