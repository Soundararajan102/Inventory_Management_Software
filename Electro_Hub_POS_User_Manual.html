Electro Hub POS Functional Manual Section 3: Customer Management & GST State Detection

03 Customer Management & GST State Routing

Customer handling in Electro Hub is designed for rapid retail throughput while maintaining full B2B GST compliance. Cashiers can complete retail sales anonymously or search and create detailed business client profiles on the fly.

Customer Selection Modes

  • Walk-in Customer (Default): Selected by default whenever the POS screen loads. Designed for fast retail sales where customer contact details or GSTIN are not required. Payments are treated as immediate Cash/UPI/Card settlement.
  • Registered Customer Search: Clicking the Customer input opens a search dropdown. Typing any 2 characters instantly searches across Customer Name, Mobile Number, or GSTIN.
  • Inline "Quick Add Customer" Modal: If a customer is not found, clicking the + New Customer button opens an inline creation dialog without losing the cart contents. Cashiers can enter:
    • Full Name (Required)
    • Phone / Mobile Number (Used for billing records and customer contacts)
    • GSTIN (15-character statutory Tax Identification Number)
    • Address & State (Used for inter-state tax routing)
    Upon saving, the new customer is instantly selected in the active bill.

Intelligent Inter-State vs Intra-State GST Auto-Routing

Electro Hub eliminates manual tax calculation errors by automatically comparing the customer's state with the active Company's state (extracted from the GSTIN or Company profile):

Transaction Type Condition Tax Splitting Applied
Intra-State Sale (Local) Customer State == Company State (or Walk-in Customer) CGST + SGST
50% Central Tax + 50% State Tax
Example on 18% GST: 9% CGST + 9% SGST
Inter-State Sale (Out of State) Customer State != Company State (e.g., Company in TN, Customer in KA) IGST (Integrated Tax)
100% Integrated Tax applied
Example on 18% GST: 18% IGST
⚠️ GST State Code Auto-Extraction
When a 15-digit GSTIN is entered for a customer (e.g., 27AAPCA1234F1Z5), the system automatically parses the first two digits (27) as the State Code (Maharashtra) and routes taxes accordingly without requiring manual state selection.

Multi-Source Split Billing ("+ Add from Godown")

A frequent operational challenge occurs when a customer needs more units than what is currently on the shop shelf, but the remaining units are stored in the warehouse.

✨ Practical Example: Multi-Godown Split Fulfillment
Scenario: A contractor visits to buy 25 units of Ceiling Fan 1200mm. The Counter Shop only has 5 units on display, while Godown A has 50 units.

Electro Hub Solution:
  1. Add Ceiling Fan 1200mm to cart. Set source to Shop with Qty = 5.
  2. Click the inline "+ Add from Godown" button beneath the item.
  3. Select Godown A and enter Qty = 20.
Both lines appear on the same invoice! The customer receives one unified A4 GST Tax Invoice for 25 fans, while the warehouse dispatch records accurately route 5 units from Counter and 20 units from Godown A.
Electro Hub POS Functional Manual Section 5: Pricing Engine, Discounts & Tax Calculations

05 Pricing Engine, Discounts & Tax Calculations

Column 2 of the POS interface houses the real-time financial calculation engine. Every price adjustment, discount, or tax setting recalculates instantly with sub-millisecond precision.

Mathematical Breakdown & Computation Order

Electro Hub follows standard Indian GST statutory rounding and computation order:

Step Calculation Component Formula & Accounting Logic
Step 1 Gross Item Amount Gross = Line Rate × Quantity for each cart item.
Step 2 Subtotal Subtotal = Σ (Gross Item Amounts)
Step 3 Invoice Discount Supports dual modes:
  • Percentage Mode (%): Discount Amount = Subtotal × (Discount % / 100)
  • Flat Rupee Mode (₹): Direct absolute rupee deduction.
Discounts are distributed pro-rata across line items to accurately calculate reduced GST taxable values.
Step 4 Taxable Value Taxable Value = Subtotal - Discount Amount
Step 5 GST Computation Computed per item HSN tax bracket:
  • Intra-State: CGST = Taxable × (GST% / 200) and SGST = Taxable × (GST% / 200)
  • Inter-State: IGST = Taxable × (GST% / 100)
Step 6 Exact Total Exact Total = Taxable Value + CGST + SGST (or IGST)
Step 7 Automatic Round-Off Net Payable = Round(Exact Total)
Rounds to the nearest whole Rupee (e.g. ₹1,450.40 becomes ₹1,450.00; ₹1,450.60 becomes ₹1,451.00). The round-off differential is printed on the invoice.

Tax-Exclusive vs Tax-Inclusive Pricing Preferences

Stores can configure their global tax method under Settings → Taxes & GST:

  • Tax Exclusive (Standard Wholesale): The entered product price represents the base cost before tax. GST is added on top of the rate during checkout.
  • Tax Inclusive (Retail MRP): The product selling price includes GST. The POS engine back-calculates the taxable base price using: Base = MRP / (1 + GST% / 100) and extracts the embedded CGST/SGST amounts automatically.

Partial Payments & Credit (Khata / Due) Workflow

In B2B wholesale and contractor billing, customers frequently make partial down payments and carry the remaining balance on credit:

📋 Partial Payment Handling
How to Record a Credit Sale:
  1. Select or create the registered Customer (Credit cannot be extended to anonymous Walk-in customers).
  2. The "Amount Paid" input box defaults to the Net Grand Total.
  3. Manually type the amount received today (e.g., Grand Total = ₹50,000; Customer pays ₹20,000 cash).
  4. The system dynamically computes: Pending Balance = ₹30,000.00.
  5. Upon clicking Save & Print:
    • ₹20,000 is credited to the Cash ledger.
    • ₹30,000 is automatically debited to the Customer's Due Khata.
    • The printed A4 Tax Invoice displays: Paid Amount: ₹20,000.00 | Balance Due: ₹30,000.00.

Electro Hub POS Functional Manual Section 9: Quotations & Estimates Lifecycle

09 Quotations & Estimates Management

In electrical contracting and commercial trade, clients frequently demand detailed price estimates before confirming purchases. Electro Hub includes a complete, end-to-end Quotation Lifecycle module directly inside POS.

Quotation Lifecycle Workflow

  1. Creating an Estimate: Click the mode switcher to toggle from Tax Invoice to Quotation. Add products, adjust custom rates or discounts, select the client, and click "Save & Print Quotation".
  2. Zero Stock Impact: The quotation is safely recorded in the database, but no inventory is deducted. The store stock remains 100% available for cash retail customers.
  3. Quotation Register Modal: Click the "📋 Quotations" button on the header bar to browse all historical quotes. Filter by:
    • Pending: Active quotes awaiting client confirmation.
    • Converted: Quotes that were confirmed and converted into Tax Invoices.
    • All: Complete chronological archive.
  4. 1-Click Conversion to Tax Invoice: When the customer returns with the purchase order:
    • Open the Quotations register and click "Convert to Invoice".
    • POS automatically switches to Tax Invoice mode and loads all items, quantities, and agreed prices into the cart.
    • The system verifies real-time inventory levels across shop and warehouses.
    • Upon finalizing the sale, the original quotation is automatically marked as "Converted" with a direct link to the new Invoice Number.
  5. Editing & Updating: Click "Edit" on any pending quotation to update quantities or negotiated pricing without creating orphan records.
Electro Hub POS Functional Manual Section 10: Standard A4 GST Tax Invoices & Quotations

10 Standard A4 GST Tax Invoices & Quotations

Electro Hub is designed exclusively around Standard A4 GST Tax Invoicing. Every invoice and quotation is formatted according to statutory Indian accounting standards and Tally invoice conventions for professional presentation, complete legal compliance, and easy archival.

Standard Tally-Compliant A4 GST Tax Invoice Architecture

The generated A4 Tax Invoice contains the following comprehensive sections:

  • Statutory 3-Tier Copy Title: Prints formal copy designations: "Original for Buyer" (Customer copy), "Duplicate for Transporter" (Transit inspection copy), or "Triplicate for Supplier" (Store accounting copy).
  • Seller (Consignor) Header: Store Name, Registered Trade Name, Address, Contact Telephone, Email, 15-digit GSTIN, State Name, and 2-digit State Code.
  • Buyer (Bill to) & Consignee (Ship to) Blocks: Full legal customer name, registered business billing address, delivery site address, GSTIN, and State Code.
  • Invoice & Logistics Summary Grid: Invoice Number, e-Way Bill Number, Invoice Date, Delivery Note reference, Mode/Terms of Payment, Dispatched through, Destination, Bill of Lading / LR-RR Number, and Motor Vehicle Number.
  • Itemized Billing Grid: Serial Number, Description of Goods, HSN/SAC Code, Quantity, Unit of Measurement (Pcs, Box, Roll, Mtr), Rate per unit, Item Discount %, and Taxable Amount.
  • Mandatory GST HSN Summary Table: A dedicated statutory tax breakdown table grouping all items by HSN code, displaying:
    • HSN / SAC Code
    • Taxable Value
    • Central Tax Rate & Amount (CGST)
    • State Tax Rate & Amount (SGST)
    • Integrated Tax Rate & Amount (IGST for interstate)
    • Total Tax Amount
  • Amount Chargeable in Words: Official Indian Rupee currency words notation (e.g. "INR Twenty Three Thousand Ten Only") for legal validity.
  • Bank Remittance Details: Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number, Branch IFSC Code, and dynamic UPI QR code for direct digital payment settlement.
  • Terms & Declaration Block: Standard commercial terms, jurisdiction clauses, interest terms on overdue balances, and Authorized Signatory seal & signature block.

Standard A4 Quotation / Estimate Voucher

When printing in Quotation Mode, the system formats a clean A4 Quotation document with full item pricing, company branding, terms of quotation validity, and signature lines while properly omitting Tax Invoice legal headers.
